# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Leads

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define quality standards and own the test strategy for projects. They validate acceptance criteria, coordinate testing workflows, and provide quality input to release decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality standards, test plans, and coverage expectations
- Validate acceptance criteria with Product Managers before implementation
- Coordinate manual and automated testing with Developers
- Track defects, quality risks, and testing blockers with Project Managers
- Recommend whether a release is ready based on test results

### Goals
- Detect defects early and reduce production issues
- Ensure consistent, measurable quality across projects
- Enable fast, confident releases

### Typical Communication
- Backlog refinement and acceptance-criteria reviews with Product Managers
- Test planning, defect triage, and daily coordination with Developers
- Quality status, risks, and release-readiness updates with Project Managers

---

## Product Leads

### Role Summary
Product Leads provide senior oversight of product strategy and major product decisions. They guide Product Managers, align priorities with business objectives, and escalate strategic risks or blockers.

### Responsibilities
- Mentor and guide Product Managers on strategy and prioritization
- Approve major roadmap, scope, and prioritization decisions
- Resolve or escalate product risks and cross-team blockers
- Represent product strategy in executive and cross-functional planning
- Align Developers and Project Managers on product trade-offs

### Goals
- Ensure product direction aligns with business objectives and customer value
- Unblock decisions and dependencies that exceed a Product Manager’s authority
- Build consistent product practices and decision-making

### Typical Communication
- Strategy and prioritization reviews with Product Managers
- Trade-off and feasibility discussions with Developers
- Roadmap, risk, and escalation updates with Project Managers and sponsors

---

## Engineering Leads/Tech Leads

### Role Summary
Engineering Leads/Tech Leads provide technical direction for projects. They own or guide architecture decisions, assess technical risks, and coordinate engineering work across teams.

### Responsibilities
- Establish and review technical approach and architecture decisions
- Assess technical risks, dependencies, and implementation trade-offs
- Support Developers with design guidance, reviews, and technical decisions
- Coordinate cross-team technical work and resolve engineering blockers
- Partner with Product Managers and Project Managers on scope, estimates, and feasibility

### Goals
- Deliver secure, maintainable, and scalable technical solutions
- Reduce technical risk and avoid costly rework
- Keep engineering work aligned with product goals and delivery commitments

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and technical planning with Developers
- Feasibility, estimates, and trade-off discussions with Product Managers
- Dependency, risk, and delivery updates with Project Managers

---

## Sponsors/Executive Stakeholders

### Role Summary
Sponsors/Executive Stakeholders provide business sponsorship and decision authority for significant initiatives. They approve budget and strategic direction, remove organizational barriers, and receive escalations on critical issues.

### Responsibilities
- Approve business cases, budget, strategic direction, and major scope changes
- Set organizational priorities and provide executive-level context
- Resolve escalated resource, dependency, and risk issues
- Review progress, outcomes, and critical decisions
- Hold Product Managers and Project Managers accountable for agreed outcomes

### Goals
- Ensure investment delivers meaningful business and customer value
- Make timely decisions on issues beyond the delivery team’s authority
- Maintain executive alignment and organizational support

### Typical Communication
- Status, budget, and critical-risk reports from Project Managers
- Strategy, outcomes, and major decision requests from Product Managers
- Major technical constraints and delivery implications from Developers
- Delivery health and significant technical risks through Project Managers and Engineering Leads

---

## Security Engineers

### Role Summary
Security Engineers ensure projects meet security requirements throughout design, development, and release. They conduct security reviews, advise delivery teams, and coordinate response to security incidents.

### Responsibilities
- Define and review security requirements and acceptance criteria
- Perform threat modeling, architecture reviews, and security testing
- Advise Developers on secure implementation and remediation
- Track security findings and risks with Project Managers
- Support incident response and communicate material security issues

### Goals
- Prevent vulnerabilities and protect customer and company data
- Integrate security into delivery rather than defer it to release
- Resolve security findings within appropriate risk and compliance thresholds

### Typical Communication
- Security requirements and threat-model reviews with Product Managers
- Design reviews, remediation guidance, and vulnerability triage with Developers
- Security risk, finding, and incident updates with Project Managers
